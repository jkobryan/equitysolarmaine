The Mission

Maine is facing an unprecedented energy affordability crisis. Electricity is a necessity, not a luxury, yet utility rates continue to climb as a share of household expenditures, especially among lower-income households.


Equity Solar was founded to help correct that disparity. Using community-based solar development and Maine's Net Energy Billing program, we provide low-priced, renewable energy to renters, resident-owned communities, and housing authorities.
