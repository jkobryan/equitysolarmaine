The Mission

Maine is facing an unprecedented energy affordability crisis. Electricity is a necessity, not a luxury, yet utility rates continue to climb as a share of household expenditures, especially among lower-income households.


Equity Solar was founded to help correct that disparity. Using community-based solar development and Maine's Net Energy Billing program, we provide low-priced, renewable energy to renters, homeowners, resident-owned communities, and housing authorities—all without the need for expensive rooftop installations.

How It Works

1. Contact Our Team

Reach out if you'd like to join an Equity Solar project. There are zero upfront costs or hidden fees.

2. We Generate, You Save

Our solar arrays feed clean power into the local CMP grid, generating credits for your account.

3. Reduced Bills

Your CMP bill is automatically reduced. You pay less for electricity while supporting local green energy.
