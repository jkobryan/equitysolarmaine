The Mission

Maine is facing an unprecedented energy affordability crisis. Electricity is a necessity, not a luxury, yet utility rates continue to climb while household budgets shrink.


Equity Solar was founded to flip the script. By leveraging community-based solar development and Maine's Net Energy Billing programs, we provide low-priced, renewable energy to renters, homeowners, and small businesses—all without the need for expensive rooftop installations.

How It Works

1. Claim Your Spot

Sign up to join an Equity Solar community project. There are zero upfront costs or hidden fees.

2. We Generate, You Save

Our Maine-based solar arrays feed clean power into the local grid, generating credits for your account.

3. Reduced Bills

Your CMP or Versant bill is automatically reduced. You pay less for electricity while supporting local green energy.
